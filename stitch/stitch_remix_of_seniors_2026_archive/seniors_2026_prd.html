# Seniors 2026: Product Requirements Document ## 1. Overview A private, modern web platform for the graduating class of 2026. A "Digital Curator" of shared memories, identity, and emotions, designed as a premium, secure community space. ## 2. Core Experience - **Vibe:** Apple modern UI + Instagram energy + cinematic graduation nostalgia. - **Access:** Private only. Admin-created accounts (`firstname.code@seniors26.ozm`). - **Interactive:** Heavy emphasis on animations, smooth transitions, and scroll-based motion. ## 3. Screen Inventory & Flow ### Phase 1: Authentication & Entry - **Login Page:** Clean, secure entrance. Fields for username and password. ### Phase 2: The Core Experience (Post-Login) - **Home Dashboard:** Welcome message, Graduation Countdown, and navigation. High emotional impact. - **Seniors Directory:** Grid of profiles (photo, nickname, quote, fun fact). Approved memories section. - **The Wall:** Masonry board of "sticky notes" for funny moments, quotes, and memes. Playful rotation and motion. - **Memory Feed:** Instagram-style vertical feed for photos and videos. Becomes a read-only archive post-graduation. - **Seniors Awards:** Voting interface (search-based selection) and a "Coming Soon" results page. - **Hall of Thanks:** Teacher appreciation cards with anonymous messaging. - **Time Capsule:** Private space for future goals and messages to one's future self. ### Phase 3: Control & Governance - **Admin Dashboard:** Comprehensive management of users, content moderation, countdown settings, and feature toggles (voting, uploads). ## 4. Visual Guidelines (Design System) - **Theme:** Light mode, warm neutrals, soft gradients. - **Typography:** Noto Serif for headers (editorial), clean Sans for body. - **UI Elements:** Large radius corners, frosted glass effects, floating cards. ## 5. Technical Requirements - Responsive (Desktop & Mobile optimized). - Admin approval workflow for all user-generated content.
